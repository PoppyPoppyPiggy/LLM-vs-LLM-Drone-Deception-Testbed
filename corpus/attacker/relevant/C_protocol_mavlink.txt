[LAYER C — protocol spec | source: MAVLink developer docs | message/command semantics by stage]

Reconnaissance messages: HEARTBEAT (0) advertises type/autopilot/mode at ~1 Hz;
PARAM_REQUEST_LIST/PARAM_VALUE enumerate parameters; GLOBAL_POSITION_INT/ATTITUDE stream
telemetry; STATUSTEXT carries status (a formulaic STATUSTEXT is a decoy tell). Use these to
fingerprint a target cheaply before committing.

Intrusion messages: COMMAND_LONG (76) carries MAV_CMD codes with an ACK; SET_MODE and
MISSION_* change behavior/upload waypoints. A real ArduPilot gates arming/mode/takeoff by
pre-arm checks and transitions consistently with physics; a decoy transitions instantly or
inconsistently. MAVLink2 signed frames (STX 0xFD, incompat flag 0x01, 13-byte signature)
are rejected without the shared key.

Exfiltration messages: harvest via companion HTTP and credential replay; on the MAVLink
side, log/parameter reads. Fake credentials that are structurally valid but never
authenticate elsewhere, or config dumps that contradict earlier telemetry, mark a honeypot.
